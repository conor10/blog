# How I use AI

All posts on this site are written by me, drafted with a workflow I've been using for years — in a markdown editor ([iA Writer](https://ia.net/) is my tool of choice). I use AI as a copy editor to catch grammatical errors, highlight structural and readability issues.

Elsewhere, including the rest of this site, I use AI tooling more freely, in the same way I would with any other work to build, assist with learning and summarise more efficiently than I can alone.

When I share my thoughts, I want them to be coming from the same place they would be if I were in the room with you — without being augmented by an LLM.
